Usage of AI

I, Lumaa, use integrated artificial intelligence to code, like Xcode's Coding intelligence and predictive code completion, or even Claude Code. The only artificial intelligence that I use is Claude sometimes with a subscription, sometimes without. This speeds up development speed, optimization, and sometimes the usage of new APIs.

Siri AI

Siri AI is Apple's artificial intelligence ran on supported Apple devices, outside of Europe and China. Apple's AppIntents API allows to fetch data, or act on the app outside of the app itself. Apple gave developers new tools in OS 26 to use their on-device large-language models (LLMs) or the Private Cloud Compute (in OS 27) in our apps to generate plain text or structured data. Additionally, developers can also prompt users to generate images using Image Playground.
