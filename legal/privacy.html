You share no information directly to Swiftseerr itself, all information in Swiftseerr are only shared to your Seerr and/or SeerrAPN instance(s). Data shared is handled by your server(s) administrators.

SeerrAPN

SeerrAPN stores devices' push notification identifier, notifications filter, Seerr account identifier and Seerr account permissions, these data are stored in plain text and are handled by your server(s) administrators. To remove your device token from the SeerrAPN server(s), simply edit the SeerrAPN URL text field in the Swiftseerr in-app settings.

If you use a different app with SeerrAPN integration, make sure to read about how to delete your device push notification identifier on their Seerr app.

Security Measures

All data received from any of Lumaa's apps to Lumaa's servers are encrypted, and decrypted only under strict conditions. If a security breach ever happens, users will be notified and all data will be erased.
