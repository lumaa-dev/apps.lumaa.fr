Swiftseerr

Request movies and TV shows using a native and open-source Seerr client.

Discoveries

Discover the trending movies and TV shows at the moment, view upcoming and popular movies as well as TV shows.


Search

Search for all the movies and TV shows that you may need... Whether it's in English, in Spanish, in black and white, in the 1950s, in the 2020s, whatever you need? It's available. Just tap on the movie/TV show cover to display more information about it.


Specific Findings

For a more linear search, you can always look for a only movies, or only TV shows, using the tab bar at the bottom of the screen.


Precisely, this

When looking at a movie or a TV show, you get all the information required to know if you'll like or not, like for example: The release date, the age rating, the duration, a little summary, the actors and crew list, etc...


Open or closed?

Thanks to SwiftUI, Swiftseerr will be instantly available on the iPhone Duo. With an adapted user interface, set on the right side of the screen. The iPhone Duo, opened or closed, is no less than any other iPhones on Swiftseerr.


SeerrAPN

Apple Push Notifications meet Seerr.

Push Notified

After setting up a SeerrAPN server, subscribe for push notifications in Swiftseerr's in-app settings. Choose to filter out the notifications you don't want, then, whenever Seerr detects activity, you'll receive the notification instantly.
